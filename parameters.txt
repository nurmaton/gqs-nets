Settings of gqs.py for the pictures of the GQS-nets in the figures with the digits 2026, the letters G, Q, S and the animals

Each net is shown at t1 (the digit, letter or animal) and at other positions of the same flexion (t2; for the animals
t2, t3, t4), from the same view and at the same zoom.

To reproduce a picture:
  1. set the parameters of the net at the top of gqs.py (or in the Params dialog) and start gqs.py;
  2. type t into the t box;
  3. type elev, azim and zoom into the view boxes;
  4. switch Shadow off and Hidden on or off, and press PNG;
  5. turn the saved picture by the angle given (gqs.py has no camera roll).

Notation: BASE_ANGLES_DEG = (alpha_1, beta_1, gamma_1, delta_1) in degrees, BASE_KN = (kappa_0, nu_0), ROW_TYPES[nu] = rho_nu,
SIGNS = (e_1, e_2, e_3, e_4), E0 = e_0.
